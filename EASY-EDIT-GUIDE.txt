GLOSSÉ — EASY EDIT GUIDE

This guide explains how to update your GLOSSÉ cosmetics website without redesigning it.

1. PRODUCT PHOTOS

Your product photos should be stored inside:

assets/products/

Use these exact image filenames:

1. blush.jpg
2. collection.jpg
3. Duo_blush.jpg
4. henna.jpg
5. kajal.jpg
6. lip_balm.jpg
7. lip_care.jpg
8. lip_gloss.jpg
9. lip_tint.jpg
10. lipstick.jpg
11. packaging.jpg

Important: The filenames in this guide must match the filenames in your products.js file exactly. Keep capital letters and underscores the same.

2. PRODUCT NAMES, PRICES AND REVIEWS

Open:

products.js

Each product has these details:

* name — product name
* category — product category
* price — product price in Sri Lankan rupees
* reviews — review count
* image — product image path

Example:

{
  name: "Lip Gloss",
  category: "LIPS",
  price: "1,250",
  reviews: "0",
  image: "assets/products/lip_gloss.jpg"
}

To change a product, edit the values inside the quotation marks.

The prices and review counts currently used in the website are sample values. Update prices to match your actual products. Only display review counts that accurately represent your customer reviews.

3. ADDING OR CHANGING PRODUCT PHOTOS

1. Open the assets/products/ folder.
2. Add your product image.
3. Make sure its filename matches the path in products.js.
4. If you use a different filename, update the corresponding image path in products.js.

For example:

image: "assets/products/lip_gloss.jpg"

4. KEEP THE FILES IN THE CORRECT PLACES

Your website files should be arranged like this:

glosse-website/
├── index.html
├── products.js
├── EASY-EDIT-GUIDE.txt
└── assets/
    └── products/
        ├── blush.jpg
        ├── collection.jpg
        ├── Duo_blush.jpg
        ├── henna.jpg
        ├── kajal.jpg
        ├── lip_balm.jpg
        ├── lip_care.jpg
        ├── lip_gloss.jpg
        ├── lip_tint.jpg
        ├── lipstick.jpg
        └── packaging.jpg

Keep index.html and products.js in the same main folder. Keep all product photos inside assets/products/.

5. OPEN YOUR WEBSITE

Your published website is:

https://glosse-website.github.io/glosse-website/

After changing files on GitHub, wait for the website to update, then refresh the page.

6. IMPORTANT BUSINESS DETAILS

Before using the website for real sales, check and update the checkout details, delivery information, policies, social media links and payment arrangements.

Make sure product information, prices and customer reviews are accurate.
